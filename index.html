What is the minimum order?

Orders start at 10 hampers. For larger quantities, share your requirement and delivery date with the team on WhatsApp.

How much does a hamper cost?

The Golden Jyoti starts at ₹670 per hamper for 50+ hampers. The Eternal Flame starts at ₹1,660 and the Aura of Diyas at ₹2,150. Final pricing depends on quantity, contents and branding.

Can we add our company logo?

Yes. Custom branding is available on the box and contents, and you can personalise the packaging too.

Can we change what goes inside?

Yes. There are over 1 lakh combinations, so you can swap items to fit your budget, your team and the occasion.
